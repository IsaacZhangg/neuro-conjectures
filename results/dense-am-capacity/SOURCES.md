# Sources

Checked 2026-11-25.

## Primary

- Lucibello, C. and Mézard, M. Exponential Capacity of Dense Associative
  Memories. Phys. Rev. Lett. 132, 077301 (2024). doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.132.077301;
  arXiv:2304.14964. Statement location: Sec. III, eqs. (9) and (10); Appendix D.2;
  Appendix F.2, eq. (86) and the sentence "It remains an open question whether
  we have α_c^lb = α_c or not."

## Model origin

- Demircigil, M., Heusel, J., Löwe, M., Upgang, S. and Vermet, F. On a model of
  associative memory with huge storage capacity. J. Stat. Phys. (2017).
  arXiv:1702.01929.
- Ramsauer, H. et al. Hopfield Networks is All You Need. ICLR 2021.
  arXiv:2008.02217. (Rigorous but weaker lower bound, Lucibello and Mézard
  Appendix E.)

## Later work that retests, refines or disputes it

- Hu, J. Y.-C., Wu, D. and Liu, H. Provably optimal memory capacity for modern
  Hopfield models (2024), arXiv:2410.23126. Tight capacity for patterns forming
  spherical codes; does not treat iid Gaussian patterns.
- Sumedha and Singh. Free energy landscape of dense associative memory (2026),
  arXiv:2607.19195. Re-derives the typical-pattern threshold only.
- Petrova, Polyachenko and State (2026), arXiv:2604.07401, and Petrova (2026),
  arXiv:2603.13350: spherical patterns, typical retrieval.
- Ota and Taki (2026), arXiv:2609.10976: phases of the Krotov–Hopfield class,
  typical Gaussian patterns.
- Cai, T., Fan, J. and Jiang, T. Distributions of angles in random packing on
  spheres. JMLR 14 (2013), arXiv:1306.0256; Cai, T. and Jiang, T. Phase
  transition in limiting distributions of coherence of high-dimensional random
  matrices. J. Multivariate Anal. 107 (2012), arXiv:1102.2926. Extreme angles
  and correlations for exponentially many points; normalized, so they do not
  give the λ = ∞ threshold (see NOVELTY.md).
- None of these addresses the all-pattern threshold for Gaussian patterns.
