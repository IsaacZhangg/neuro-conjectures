# Sources (checked 2026-11-25)

- W. Krauth and M. Mézard, "Storage capacity of memory networks with binary couplings",
  J. Phys. France 50 (1989). The prediction α⋆(κ).
- J. Ding and N. Sun, "Capacity lower bound for the Ising perceptron", arXiv 1809.07742;
  PTRF 193 (2025) 627–715. Lower bound at κ = 0, conditional on a one-variable condition.
- B. Huang, "Capacity threshold for the Ising perceptron", arXiv 2404.18902. Upper bound at
  every κ, conditional on Conditions 1.3, 3.1, 3.3, 3.4 (and Claim 2.6, verified at κ = 0).
- Y. Shmalo, "The storage capacity of the Ising perceptron: verification of the outstanding
  numerical conditions", arXiv 2607.11010 (July 2026). Certifies all conditions at κ = 0, so the
  κ = 0 capacity is settled.
- Y. Shmalo, "The Ising perceptron at nonzero margin: certified bounds along the
  replica-symmetric curve", Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.24181576, v2.1 (Nov 13, 2026); earlier v2
  Zenodo 24147531 (Nov 12, 2026). Certifies Huang's upper bound at κ = −0.45, −0.05, 0.05,
  0.0995, 0.13; three of the four conditions on κ ∈ [−0.65, 0.19]. States that the lower bound
  α⋆(κ) at κ ≠ 0 is open. Only the Zenodo abstracts were read.
- S. Nakajima, "Uniqueness of replica-symmetric saddle point for Ising perceptron",
  arXiv 2512.23195. Uniqueness of the RS saddle for κ ≥ 0.
- C. Xu (2019) and S. Nakajima, N. Sun, arXiv 2204.03469: sharp threshold and universality,
  which upgrade positive-probability statements to high probability.

## What is open (as of 2026-11-25)

The lower bound capacity ≥ α⋆(κ) at every κ ≠ 0, and the upper bound at κ > 0.19
(no certification found beyond κ = 0.13 for all four conditions).
